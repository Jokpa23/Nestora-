Our Story

From one office on Admiralty Way

Nestora began in 2008 with two consultants, a filing cabinet and a firm rule: never list a property we hadn't walked through ourselves. In a market where listings are often copied and re-posted without verification, that rule turned out to be the whole business.

Today we advise buyers, sellers, landlords and institutional investors across Lekki, Ikoyi, Victoria Island, Ajah and Ikeja. Our team has grown to 34 people — consultants, valuers, a legal desk and a property management unit — but the discipline hasn't changed. Every listing on this website has been inspected, priced against real comparables and documented.

We are deliberately a Lagos specialist. We would rather know five corridors exceptionally well than claim to cover a country.

Our growth

Eighteen years, one city

A few milestones from the Nestora story — the moments that shaped how we work today.

2008

Nestora opens in Lekki Phase 1

Two consultants, one office and a promise to personally inspect every property before it is listed. The first year closes with 26 lettings.

2012

Sales division and legal desk launched

We bring title verification and conveyancing support in-house so buyers get clean documentation without chasing three separate vendors.

2016

Property management unit founded

Landlords hand us the keys; we handle tenant vetting, rent collection, maintenance and reporting. It now manages over 300 units.

2019

Ikoyi and Victoria Island coverage

Two new advisory teams open on the Island, extending our reach to prime residential and commercial stock.

2022

Investment advisory practice

We start publishing quarterly price-per-square-metre notes and advising diaspora clients on Lagos land acquisitions.

2026

1,240 verified listings and counting

A digital-first portfolio, 34 people and a 4.9 average client rating across Google and PropertyPro reviews.
